You are connected to SIOS, an operating system built for AI. Read the sios://briefing resource (or call sios_briefing) first: it lists this computer's files with labels, people, off-limits places and what every action costs. To get something done, call sios_do with the whole goal in plain words. SIOS plans it, checks every step with its permission broker, and the owner approves once on their screen. Don't ask for files or paths step by step; one sios_do is faster and safer. If a goal is still waiting for the owner, call sios_goal_status.
