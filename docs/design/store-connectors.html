Store: Connectors

Mockup only, for the owner's approval (C3, D105). Names and tools are examples. Connectors are services and tools an AI can use (MCP servers). Each one says who makes it, where it connects, and what each of its tools can do, before anything is installed. Installing and choosing who can use it ask for your password.

1. Browse

Search and categories like the app store. Every card shows the publisher, a Verified badge when SIOS checked it, and a risk summary of its tools. Local connectors run on this computer in a locked container; online ones talk to one service you can see.

2. A connector's page

Before installing: who makes it, the one address it talks to, and every tool with what it can do. After: who can use it (SIOS, your paired AI apps, your agents), each on its own switch. If the service changes a tool, SIOS switches the connector off and shows what changed, so it can't quietly gain powers.
